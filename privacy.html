Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What data is accessed

APP_NAME requests read-only access to the owner's Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and the account email address.

How data is used

Calendar data is used only to show and summarize the owner's own events inside a personal assistant running on the owner's private server.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising or for training generalized AI/ML models.

Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
